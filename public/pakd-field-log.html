How to use this
In person: walk in, find a staff member, ask: "If I ordered something here and authorised someone else to pick it up on my behalf — with my order number and a written authority — would your policy allow that?" Log the answer.

Online: search "[store name] click and collect someone else" — check their FAQ or help centre, log what their published policy says, and paste the URL.

The timing question matters most:
  • Anytime / barcode — collector named after ordering, or just shows the collection barcode → perfect for Pakd
  • After order — phone the store / edit the order to nominate → works for Pakd
  • Checkout only — nominee locked when ordering, can't change → parked for now
